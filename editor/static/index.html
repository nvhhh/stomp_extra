Connect your pedal

This hosted editor uses the Stomp Bridge on your PC for USB MIDI and NAM imports. Your presets and captures stay on your computer.

From the project folder, start the bridge:

.venv/Scripts/python editor/server.py --pages

Close other pedal editors first. Use Chrome or Edge and allow local network access when prompted.
